micook related

When you sign up to be a micook you are technically transforming into a food business. Go you! As part of this process, you need to take a few simple steps to cover yourself in terms of operating a food business in your own home (this is part of the Food Act 2003). Here’s how you do that, step by step. This may seem like a long way to get what you want, but the good news is you only have to do it once and you can cook and earn a living from home. It’s worth it! You can do this! However, if you have any further questions or concerns please contact mitabl here.

  1. Get an ABN (Australian Business Number) here.
  2. Apply for a Food Safety Supervisory Certificate.
  3. Obtain development consent by following the steps below.
  4. Finally, register with your local council under the Food Act. Once the occupation certificate is obtained, you (the new food business) will need to register with your local council. Contact your local council and ask for the appropriate forms to fill out, and they will provide them to you. If you have any further questions or concerns please contact mitabl here.

    How to obtain development consent:
    • Lodge a development application with Council for the use of the site and any required fit out. Depending on the intensity they may need to fit out to Australian Standard 1547 or Food standards code 3.2.3.
      • a. Once consent is obtained obtain a construction certificate (CC) and undertake the required works.
      • b. Once works are completed, have the required certification done of the works and apply for an occupation certificate (OC).
    • Engage a private certifier to obtain a complying development certificate or CDC. Fit out must comply with Australian standard 1547 which is more onerous than Food standards code 3.2.3.
      • a. Once plans are submitted obtain have the certifier, issue a Notice Of Commencement (NOC) and undertake works as needed.
      • b. Once works are completed, have the required certification done of the works and apply for an occupation certificate (OC).
