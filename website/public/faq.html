Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about discovering and enjoying local food with mitabl.

Platform Basics

What is mitabl?
mitabl is a mobile-first marketplace that connects local micooks with nearby food lovers.

Where does the experience happen?
The mobile app is where customers browse menus, place orders, and manage their account.

For Foodies

What can I do in the app?
Discover local kitchens, explore menus, place secure orders, and leave reviews after your meal.

Can I order directly from the website?
No. The website is for marketing and information, while ordering is managed in the mitabl app.

For micooks

What can micooks manage?
micooks can maintain kitchen profiles, update menus, and manage order fulfilment through mitabl platform workflows.

How is day-to-day reliability supported?
mitabl's backend operations support order tracking, payment flow, and operational visibility for trusted service delivery.

Support

How do I get help?
Support is handled by mitabl's customer operations team through internal service tools.

Where is customer data managed?
Customer and order data are managed by mitabl backend systems that power the app experience.
